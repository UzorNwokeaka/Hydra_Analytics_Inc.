# GDPR Employee Data Privacy Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes the organisation’s requirements for protecting personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Data Collection Principles

Personal data must be collected lawfully, fairly, and transparently. The organisation shall only collect personal information that is necessary for legitimate business and compliance purposes.

## Data Accuracy

Employees responsible for maintaining personal data records must ensure that all personal information is accurate, complete, and kept up to date where necessary.

## Access Control

Access to personal data shall be restricted to authorised personnel only. Employees must not access or share personal information without appropriate business justification and approval.

## Data Retention

Personal data shall only be retained for as long as necessary to fulfil legal, regulatory, and operational requirements. Expired records must be securely deleted or anonymised.

## Data Security

The organisation shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

## Data Breach Reporting

Any suspected personal data breach must be reported immediately to the Data Protection Officer and investigated in accordance with internal incident management procedures.

## Employee Responsibilities

All employees are required to complete annual data protection training and comply with this policy when handling personal information.
