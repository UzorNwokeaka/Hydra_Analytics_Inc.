# Anti-Money Laundering Customer Due Diligence Policy

## Purpose

This policy outlines the organisation’s anti-money laundering (AML) customer due diligence requirements to prevent financial crime and comply with regulatory obligations.

## Customer Identification

Before establishing a business relationship, employees must verify the identity of all customers using reliable and independent documentation.

## Beneficial Ownership

The organisation must identify and verify the beneficial owners of corporate customers and assess ownership structures to detect potential financial crime risks.

## Risk Assessment

Customers shall be assessed according to risk factors including jurisdiction, transaction behaviour, politically exposed person status, and business activities.

## Enhanced Due Diligence

Enhanced due diligence procedures must be applied to high-risk customers, including additional identity verification and ongoing monitoring.

## Ongoing Monitoring

Customer transactions must be continuously monitored to identify suspicious behaviour, unusual transaction patterns, or potential money laundering activity.

## Suspicious Activity Reporting

Employees are required to report suspicious transactions or unusual customer activity immediately to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer.

## Record Retention

AML compliance records, customer identification documents, and transaction monitoring evidence must be retained in accordance with applicable regulatory retention periods.
